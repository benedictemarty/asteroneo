# Jaquette AsteroNeo — prompt de génération

Image : [`dist/cover.jpg`](../dist/cover.jpg), générée le 2026-09-16 avec
Google Gemini (génération d'image) à partir du prompt ci-dessous. Style
visé : jaquette cassette britannique 1987 (Superior Software / Acornsoft).
L'illustration ne reproduit pas le rendu vectoriel du jeu (choix assumé,
dans l'esprit des jaquettes de l'époque).

```
Vintage 1987 8-bit home computer game cassette inlay cover, painted sci-fi
illustration in the style of British Superior Software / Acornsoft box art.
Portrait orientation, cassette J-card proportions.

Subject: a lone sleek arrowhead-shaped starfighter, seen from behind at a
dramatic three-quarter angle, engines blazing, banking hard through a dense
field of huge jagged tumbling asteroids in deep space; its laser bolt shatters
a giant rock into glowing fragments. Above, a menacing flying saucer with a
domed cockpit fires a beam at the ship; a smaller, faster saucer darts in from
the side. In the background, the hero ship's hyperspace jump leaves a
shimmering blue-white distortion. Distant stars, a huge ringed planet and a
purple nebula fill the void. Epic, perilous arcade mood.

Hand-painted airbrush/gouache retro sci-fi style, rich saturated colours
(deep blues, purples, orange-red engine glow), strong rim lighting, cinematic,
1980s aesthetic, no photorealism, no modern digital look.

At the TOP, an ornate chrome-and-gold sci-fi title logo reading "AsteroNeo".
Below it a smaller ribbon banner reading "Arcade Game".
Across the BOTTOM, a solid colour strip reading "Neo6502"
```

Pistes d'itération (non réalisées) : vaisseau « simple triangular wedge
ship » pour coller au jeu ; J-card complète (face + dos + tranche).
